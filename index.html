The replacement itself takes 60–90 minutes. Add ADAS camera recalibration where your vehicle needs it, and you're usually looking at about two hours door to door — done in your driveway or at our shop, your choice.

Not immediately — the urethane adhesive that bonds your windshield needs time to cure before the glass is structurally safe. We use premium fast-cure adhesives, so the safe drive-away time is typically about one hour (it varies with temperature and humidity). If your car has lane-keep assist, automatic emergency braking, or a front camera, that camera is usually mounted to the windshield.
